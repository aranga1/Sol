Set up Sol
in 6 steps.

Takes about 20 minutes. You'll need a Mac, an iPhone, and an Obsidian vault.

1

Run the installer

One command clones the repo, creates the Python environment, and installs the macOS daemon. Estimated time: ~12 minutes including model download.

curl -L https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aranga1/Sol/main/setup/install.sh | bash
2

Point Sol at your vault

When the installer asks for your vault path, find your Obsidian vault folder. It's usually at iCloud Drive → Obsidian → [your vault name]. Paste the full path.

Sol configures your daemon

The installer registers Sol as a macOS background service via launchd. It starts automatically with your Mac — no sol start command required.

4

Connect via Tailscale

Tailscale gives your iPhone a secure private connection to your Mac — even over mobile data. Install it on both devices and sign in with the same account. Free for personal use.

5

Connect your iPhone

Run sol connect in Terminal. It prints a QR code with your connection details. Open Sol on your iPhone and tap Scan.

sol connect
6

Ask your first question

Type or speak. Sol searches your vault with semantic search powered by Ollama and answers in plain language — with links back to the source notes.
