Set up Sol
in 5 steps.

Takes about 20 minutes. You'll need a Mac, an iPhone, and an Obsidian vault.

1

Run the installer

One command clones the repo, creates the Python environment, and installs the macOS daemon. Estimated time: ~12 minutes including model download.

$ curl -L https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aranga1/Sol/main/setup/install.sh | bash
→ Checking dependencies…
→ Pulling phi3.5 model (2.2 GB)…
→ Installing daemon service…
✓ Sol installed successfully
curl -L https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aranga1/Sol/main/setup/install.sh | bash
2

Point Sol at your vault

The installer asks for your Obsidian vault path. It's the folder you see in iCloud Drive → Obsidian — usually named after your vault.

▼ iCloud Drive
▼ Obsidian
📂 Sol
~/Library/Mobile Documents/…/Sol
3

Start the daemon

The daemon runs in the background on your Mac. It indexes your vault, handles queries via Ollama, and auto-restarts after reboots.

Listening on :8765
sol start
4

Connect your iPhone

The installer prints a QR code containing your Mac's Tailscale IP and API key. Open Sol on your iPhone and tap Scan to connect over your local network — no typing required.

✓
5

Ask your first question

Type or speak. Sol searches your vault with semantic search powered by Ollama and answers in plain language — with links back to the source notes.
